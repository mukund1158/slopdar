# Slopdar — Branding

## Name

**Slopdar** — *slop + radar* (a radar for AI slop).

Why it won:
- "Slop" is the current internet word for AI-generated junk → instant recognition.
- Short, coined, ownable, memeable ("ran it through Slopdar 💀").
- Looks great on a share card ("SLOP SCORE: 87 · detected by Slopdar").

## Tagline

**"Is it built or is it slop?"**

## Domain

**slopdar.com** (locked — `.com` for trust + reflex typing + share-card credibility).
Optionally grab `slopdar.fun` cheap and redirect to `.com`.

## Full identity block

> # Slopdar 🛰️
> ### Is it built or is it slop?
> *Paste any link. We scan it for AI & vibe-coding tells and give it a Slop Score.*

## The score name

**Slop Score** (0–100).

## Tiers (with personality)

- 🛠️ Hand-Crafted
- ✨ Suspiciously Clean
- 🌊 Vibe-Coded
- 🤖 Pure Slop

## Voice

Playful, meme-y, a little roast-y. Honest about being "for fun" (detects signals,
not proof). Self-aware footer joke: *"Yes, Slopdar runs on the slop stack. We know. 🌊"*
